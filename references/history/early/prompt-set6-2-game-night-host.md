## 0. Goal

Build a 12-slide Canva presentation (1920 × 1080) called **"Host the Ultimate Game Night"**. It is a playful party playbook: why game night is fun, how to plan it, five easy games, house rules, snacks, and awards.

**Reference (direction only):** https://www.canva.com/templates/EAHCe2mdq9o/ ("Yellow Pink and Blue Playful Board Game Night Presentation").

- **Borrow the idea:**
  - a bright yellow + pink + blue playful energy
  - chunky, friendly titles
  - fun game objects as decoration
- **Build from scratch.** Do not copy any layout, illustration, or text from the reference.

**Must also differ from the earlier game-night decks in this project:**
- "Game Night: The Ultimate Quiz Party" (night-time quiz show, Midnight background, Bungee, A/B/C/D answer buttons)
- "Game Night: Family Trivia Showdown" (board-game tabletop, tile path, pawns, fox host, stitched game-card banners, Titan One)

## 1. Borrow vs. must differ

| Borrow | Must differ |
|---|---|
| Yellow + pink + blue energy | The palette adds **mint, grape and tangerine**, so slides show 4+ colors. Backgrounds rotate through **4 light tints** |
| Chunky friendly titles | Titles sit on **die-cut sticker shapes** (thick white outline, soft drop shadow, slight tilt) |
| Game objects as decor | A **"sticker-bomb party"** concept: die-cut stickers, playing-card suit confetti (♥ ♦ ♣ ♠), ticket stubs, a spinner wheel, halftone pop dots |
| — | **No** board-game tabletop or winding tile path, **no** pawns as heroes, **no** night quiz-show stage, **no** A/B/C/D answer buttons, **no** brand-name games or logos |

## 2. Design system

### Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Sunny Yellow | `#FFD93B` | Accent, Game 03 |
| Bubblegum Pink | `#FF5FA2` | Titles' stickers, Game 01 |
| Pool Blue | `#3BA8FF` | Accent, Game 02 |
| Mint Pop | `#3EDDB0` | Accent, Game 04 |
| Grape | `#8B5CF6` | Accent, Game 05 |
| Tangerine | `#FF8A34` | Accent, warnings, prizes |
| Ink | `#1E1B3A` | Text |
| Paper White | `#FFFDF7` | Cards |

**Background tints** (rotate in this order, slide 1 → 12):
Butter `#FFF4C2` → Blush `#FFE0EE` → Sky `#DDF0FF` → Mint `#DDFBF1` → repeat.

**Color rules:**
- Each game slide uses its game color for the title sticker, ticket stub and card outlines:

  | Game | Name | Color |
  |---|---|---|
  | 01 | Charades | Bubblegum Pink |
  | 02 | Draw & Guess | Pool Blue |
  | 03 | Classic Card Games | Sunny Yellow |
  | 04 | Word Games | Mint Pop |
  | 05 | Bingo Bash | Grape |

- Every slide has suit confetti and halftone dots in 3+ palette colors.

### Fonts

| Role | Font | Fallback | Size | Color / style |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Titles | Luckiest Guy | Lilita One | 76–92 | Ink or Paper White, on a die-cut sticker |
| Body | Poppins Medium | Nunito | 26–28 | Ink |
| Eyebrow | Poppins ExtraBold | Nunito ExtraBold | 22 | UPPERCASE, letter spacing 200, game color |

### Recurring motifs

1. **Die-cut sticker title:** a rounded blob or star sticker in the slide's color, 10 px white outline, soft shadow, tilted −3° to 3°.
2. **Ticket stub:** a perforated ticket shape with `GAME 01`–`GAME 05`.
3. **Suit confetti:** small ♥ ♦ ♣ ♠ shapes in 4 colors scattered at the edges.
4. **Spinner wheel:** a 6-color game spinner (Canva element) used on the cover, the agenda and the awards slide.
5. **Info chips:** small rounded chips for `Players`, `Time` and `You need`.
6. **Footer:** page number on a small die with matching pips color.

**Transition:** Pop (or Slide if Pop is unavailable).

### Illustration style

- **One** flat, bold, sticker-style party set: diverse friends and families laughing, acting, drawing, playing cards, eating snacks, holding trophies.
- The asset scout searches terms like:
  - "game night illustration"
  - "friends playing cards flat"
  - "party sticker"
  - "charades illustration"
- **One** chunky outlined icon set.

## 3. Slide-by-slide spec

### Builder A

**Slide 1 — Cover** (Butter)
- Eyebrow: `YOUR PARTY PLAYBOOK`
- Title on a big Bubblegum sticker, at (120, 220), width 960, 2 lines:
  > Host the Ultimate Game Night
- Pill (Pool Blue):
  > Snacks, laughs & friendly competition.
- Presenter line:
  > Presented by [Your Name] · [Organization]
- Hero (~860, right): a group of diverse friends around a table playing cards and laughing, with confetti and a spinner wheel.
- Decor: suit confetti, halftone dots, 3 small stickers (`FUN!`, `GO!`, `WIN!`).

**Slide 2 — Why Game Night?** (Blush)
- Title: `Why Game Night?`
- Four sticker cards in a row, each a different color, with an icon:
  - `Bonding` — Laughter brings people closer.
  - `Screen-free fun` — Talk and play face to face.
  - `Brain boost` — Strategy, memory and quick thinking.
  - `All ages` — Kids to grandparents can join in.
- Hero (~360, bottom right): a grandparent and child high-fiving over a card game.

**Slide 3 — Your Planning Checklist** (Sky)
- Title: `Your Planning Checklist`
- Six checklist tickets (2 columns), each with a colored check circle:
  - `Pick a date & send invites`
  - `Choose 3–4 games for different moods`
  - `Set up a comfy table & good lighting`
  - `Prepare snacks & drinks`
  - `Make teams & a scoreboard`
  - `Get small prizes ready`
- Hero (~420): a colorful invitation card and a phone with a party emoji message.

### Builder B

**Slide 4 — Game 01: Charades** (Mint bg, Bubblegum game color)
- Ticket `GAME 01` · Eyebrow `ACT IT OUT` · Title `Charades`
- Info chips: `Players: 4+` · `Time: 20 min` · `You need: word cards, a timer`
- Card **How to play:**
  > One player acts out a word without speaking. Their team guesses before the 60-second timer runs out.
- Card **Tip:**
  > Use fun categories like movies, animals or jobs.
- Hero (~540): a person acting like a flapping bird while friends guess and laugh.

**Slide 5 — Game 02: Draw & Guess** (Butter bg, Pool Blue game color)
- Ticket `GAME 02` · Eyebrow `SKETCH IT` · Title `Draw & Guess`
- Info chips: `Players: 4+` · `Time: 20 min` · `You need: paper or a whiteboard, markers, a timer`
- Card **How to play:**
  > One player draws a secret word — no letters or numbers allowed! Their team guesses before time runs out.
- Card **Tip:**
  > Silly drawings make the best memories.
- Hero (~540): a kid drawing a cat on a whiteboard while family members shout guesses.

**Slide 6 — Game 03: Classic Card Games** (Blush bg, Sunny Yellow game color)
- Ticket `GAME 03` · Eyebrow `SHUFFLE UP` · Title `Classic Card Games`
- Info chips: `Players: 2–6` · `Time: 15 min a round` · `You need: a standard deck of cards`
- Three mini cards with fanned-card icons:
  - `Go Fish` — Collect sets of four by asking other players for cards.
  - `Crazy Eights` — Match the color or number; eights are wild.
  - `Snap` — Shout "Snap!" first when two matching cards appear.
- Hero (~460): hands fanning colorful playing cards with suit confetti.

### Builder C

**Slide 7 — Game 04: Word Games** (Sky bg, Mint Pop game color)
- Ticket `GAME 04` · Eyebrow `THINK FAST` · Title `Word Games`
- Info chips: `Players: 3+` · `Time: 10–15 min` · `You need: nothing but your brain!`
- Two cards:
  - **Word Chain:** Say a word that starts with the last letter of the previous word — no repeats!
  - **20 Questions:** One player thinks of something; others ask up to 20 yes-or-no questions to guess it.
- Hero (~500): friends with colorful speech bubbles full of letters.

**Slide 8 — Game 05: Bingo Bash** (Mint bg, Grape game color)
- Ticket `GAME 05` · Eyebrow `MARK IT` · Title `Bingo Bash`
- Info chips: `Players: 3+` · `Time: 20 min` · `You need: bingo cards, markers`
- Card **How to play:**
  > A caller reads out numbers. Players mark them, and the first to complete a line shouts "Bingo!"
- Card **Tip:**
  > Make picture bingo cards so young kids can play too.
- Hero (~500): a colorful bingo card with dabber marks and a happy winner throwing confetti.

**Slide 9 — House Rules** (Butter)
- Title: `House Rules`
- Six die-cut stickers in a 3 × 2 grid, each a different color and tilt, with an icon:
  - `Play fair`
  - `Cheer for every team`
  - `Take turns`
  - `Phones down during rounds`
  - `Be a good sport`
  - `Fun first, winning second`
- Hero (~320): a referee-whistle mascot sticker giving a thumbs up.

### Builder D

**Slide 10 — Snack Station** (Blush)
- Title: `Snack Station`
- Four round "plate" cards, each in a different color, with a food illustration and one line:

  - `Mini Sandwiches` — Easy to grab between rounds.
  - `Fruit Skewers` — Colorful, fresh and mess-free.
  - `Fizzy Lemonade` — A fun mocktail for everyone.

**Slide 11 — Scoreboard & Awards** (Sky)

  - `Best Actor`
  - `Speed Guesser`
  - `Card Shark`
  - `Good Sport`
- Decor: the spinner wheel, a trophy illustration (~260).

**Slide 12 — Thanks for Playing!** (Mint)
- Title on a big Bubblegum star sticker, Luckiest Guy 130:

  - `123-456-7890`

- Hero along the bottom (~1300): friends cheering with a trophy, cards and confetti flying.
