## 0. Goal

Build a 12-slide Canva presentation, 1920 × 1080, titled:

**"Seminar Proposal – The Influence of Social Media Use on University Students' Study Habits"**

It is an undergraduate research proposal presentation (seminar proposal / sempro).

**Reference (direction only):** https://www.canva.com/templates/EAGWIdcO9X0/ ("Presentasi Seminar Proposal Modern Merah Muda Putih")

- **Borrow these ideas:**
  - the academic proposal flow: background → problem → objectives → benefits → methodology → methods → conclusion → contact
  - floating "info badges" (small stat/icon cards) that overlap visuals
- **Build from scratch.**

## 1. Borrow vs. must differ

| Borrow | Must differ |
|---|---|
| Academic proposal flow | A colorful **"research board"** look: pinned sticky notes, index cards, and doodled arrows on a light dotted-paper background. **No** website-style top nav bar ("Beranda / Tentang / Layanan") |
| Floating info badges | Badges hold **illustrated icons and simple charts**. **No** stock photos of meetings or seminars |
| Bold titles | Rounded geometric titles in **color**, not black condensed caps |
| — | **No** pink-lavender gradient bars in the corners, **no** "Mulai Presentasi" button |

## 2. Design system

### Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper Dot | `#F8F7FF` | Background (+ a dotted-grid Canva pattern at 15 %) |
| Ink | `#24213D` | Body text |
| Violet | `#6C4CF1` | Titles, primary |
| Coral | `#FF6B6B` | Problem / attention |
| Mint | `#20C997` | Objectives / results |
| Sunshine | `#FFC83D` | Sticky notes, highlights |
| Sky | `#3BA7FF` | Method / data |
| Peach | `#FFB38A` | Soft accents |

**Card colors:**
- Sticky-note and index-card fills rotate through Sunshine, Mint, Sky, Coral, and Peach tints.
- Each card has a small colored **pushpin or washi-tape** element (Canva).

### Fonts

- **Titles:** Lexend ExtraBold (fallback: Poppins ExtraBold), Violet, 68–80.
- **Body:** Nunito, 26–28, Ink.
- **Eyebrow:** Nunito ExtraBold 22, UPPERCASE, letter spacing 200, in the section color.

### Recurring motifs

1. **Chapter tag:** a sticky-note shape at the top left, rotated −4°, e.g. `CHAPTER 1 · INTRODUCTION`.
2. **Index cards:** radius 16, slight rotation (−2° to 2°), each with a pushpin.
3. **Doodled arrows and underlines** (Canva hand-drawn elements) connecting ideas.
4. **Info badges:** small white cards with a shadow, an icon, and a short label, overlapping the hero.
5. **Footer:** `[University Name] · Seminar Proposal 2026` at the bottom left, and the page number at the bottom right. Nunito 18, slate color.

**Transition:** Fade.

### Illustration style

- One flat illustration set of diverse university students: studying, using phones, at a library, with laptops.
- Plus flat academic icons: book, chart, magnifier, calendar, survey clipboard.

## 3. Slide-by-slide spec

### Builder A

**Slide 1 — Cover**
- Eyebrow `SEMINAR PROPOSAL`.
- Title at (120, 220), width 980, 3 lines:
  > The Influence of Social Media Use on University Students' Study Habits
- Name card (index card, Sunshine):
  - `Presented by: [Student Name] · [Student ID]`
  - `Supervisor: [Supervisor Name]`
  - `[Faculty], [University Name] · 2026`
- Hero at (1060, 140), ~780: a student at a desk with a phone and books, on a big Violet-tint blob.
- Two info badges overlap the hero:
  - `📱 Social media`
  - `📚 Study habits`
  - Use Canva icons, not emoji.
- Decor: doodle arrows and stars.

**Slide 2 — Outline**
- Title: `Outline`.
- 8 sticky notes in a 4 × 2 grid. Each has a different color, a number, and a short label:
  1. Background
  2. Problem Statement
  3. Objectives
  4. Significance
  5. Literature Review
  6. Hypothesis
  7. Methodology
  8. Timeline
- Hero: a pinned corkboard doodle or a student pointing (~360).

**Slide 3 — Background (Chapter 1)**
- Tag: `CHAPTER 1 · INTRODUCTION`.
- Title: `Research Background`.
- Left: an index card, Sky:
  > Social media is part of students' daily lives. It can support learning through sharing and discussion, but it can also distract from focused study time.
- Right: hero of a student surrounded by floating app-notification icons (~600).
- Bottom: three info badges:
  - `Daily use`
  - `Learning tool?`
  - `Or distraction?`

### Builder B

**Slide 4 — Problem Statement**
- Title: `Problem Statement`.
- Hero: a large question-mark illustration with a thinking student (~480).
- Three Coral-tint index cards, numbered RQ1–RQ3:
  - `RQ1` — How intensively do university students use social media?
  - `RQ2` — What are the study habits of university students?
  - `RQ3` — Does social media use significantly influence students' study habits?

**Slide 5 — Research Objectives**
- Title: `Research Objectives`.
- Three Mint-tint cards, each with a target icon and a sticker number:
  1. To describe the intensity of students' social media use.
  2. To describe students' study habits.
  3. To analyze the influence of social media use on students' study habits.
- Hero: an archer target with an arrow in the bullseye, plus a student cheering (~420).

**Slide 6 — Significance of the Study**
- Title: `Significance of the Study`.
- Two columns:
  - **`THEORETICAL`** (Sky card): Adds evidence to studies on digital media and learning behavior.
  - **`PRACTICAL`** (Peach card), with ✓ bullets:
    - ✓ Students: build healthier digital study habits
    - ✓ Lecturers: design better learning strategies
    - ✓ University: plan digital-wellbeing programs
- Hero: a lightbulb with books (~380), placed between the columns.

### Builder C

**Slide 7 — Literature Review (Chapter 2)**
- Tag: `CHAPTER 2 · LITERATURE REVIEW`.
- Title: `Theoretical Framework`.
- Center diagram:
  - Left: a Violet rounded box, `X · Social Media Use`, with indicators: duration, frequency, purpose.
  - A thick doodled arrow points to the right.
  - Right: a Mint rounded box, `Y · Study Habits`, with indicators: time management, concentration, review routine.
- Above the arrow, a Sunshine sticky note: `Influence?`.
- Decor: small icons (clock, phone, book) on each box. A student illustration at the bottom-right (~320).

**Slide 8 — Hypothesis**
- Title: `Research Hypothesis`.
- Two big index cards, with a balance-scale illustration (~360) between them:
  - Coral card, `H₀`: There is no significant influence of social media use on students' study habits.
  - Mint card, `H₁`: There is a significant influence of social media use on students' study habits.

**Slide 9 — Methodology (Chapter 3)**
- Tag: `CHAPTER 3 · METHODOLOGY`.
- Title: `Research Method`.
- Four Sky/Sunshine/Mint/Peach cards in a 2 × 2 grid, each with an icon:

| Card | Text |
|---|---|
| `Approach` | Quantitative, survey design |
| `Population & Sample` | `[Number]` active students, sampled with `[technique]` |
| `Instrument` | Likert-scale questionnaire |
| `Data Analysis` | Validity & reliability tests, then simple linear regression |

- Hero: a clipboard survey with a pencil and a bar chart (~360).

### Builder D

**Slide 10 — Research Timeline**
- Title: `Research Timeline`.
- A Gantt chart built from plain shapes, with colorful bars across 6 month columns, `[Month 1]` … `[Month 6]`. Rows:
  1. Proposal & seminar
  2. Instrument testing
  3. Data collection
  4. Data analysis
  5. Writing results
  6. Final defense
- Hero: a calendar with a student checking items (~320), at the top-right.

**Slide 11 — Expected Results**
- Title: `Expected Results`.
- Three Mint cards:
  - A clear picture of students' social media habits
  - Evidence on whether social media affects study habits
  - Practical tips for healthier digital study routines
- Hero: a student celebrating with a graph going up (~460).
- Speech bubble: `Ready for your feedback!`

**Slide 12 — Thank You / Q&A**
- Title, centered:
  > Thank You!
- Subtitle:
  > Questions, suggestions and feedback are welcome.
- Contact pills, Violet / Sky / Mint:
  - `[email@university.ac.id]`
  - `[Student ID]`
  - `[Faculty / Study Program]`
- Hero along the bottom: students and a lecturer in discussion, illustrated (~1200 wide).
- Decor: sticky notes, doodle stars, confetti.
