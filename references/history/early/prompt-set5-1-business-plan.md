## 0. Goal

Build a 12-slide Canva presentation, 1920 × 1080, titled **"Bloom & Brew – Business Plan"**.

It is a business plan for a fictional startup: **Bloom & Brew**, a plant-filled coffee shop and co-working space for students and remote workers.

**Reference (direction only):** https://www.canva.com/templates/EAHLygh0z_E/ ("Biru, Ungu, dan Pink Modern Perencanaan Bisnis").

- **Borrow the idea:**
  - a clean business-plan flow: opportunity → market → model → marketing → finance → structure → legal/action plan;
  - rounded content frames;
  - numbered list pills;
  - small sparkle accents.
- **Build from scratch.**

## 1. Borrow vs. must differ

| Borrow | Must differ |
|---|---|
| Business-plan section flow | **Vivid pop palette** (electric blue, tangerine, lime, magenta, sunshine) on off-white. Not soft pastel lavender/pink/baby-blue |
| Rounded content frames | **Flat / isometric business illustrations** (people, café, charts, rockets). **No** stock photos of people in suits |
| Numbered pill lists | Numbered **sticker badges** in different colors, not outlined pills |
| Sparkle accents | Memphis-style shapes (squiggles, dots, half-circles) as decor. **No** offset lavender rectangles behind photos, **no** small company-name pill in the top-left corner |

## 2. Design system

### Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#FBF9F4` | Background |
| Ink Navy | `#1B1F3B` | Titles, body |
| Electric Blue | `#3D5AFE` | Primary accent |
| Tangerine | `#FF7A2F` | Accent, highlights |
| Lime | `#A3E635` | Growth / positive |
| Magenta | `#E83F9C` | Accent |
| Sunshine | `#FFCB2F` | Badges, stars |
| Mint Leaf | `#2DBE8C` | Café / plants theme |

**Card colors:** card fills rotate through 15 % tints of Blue, Tangerine, Lime and Magenta. Badge colors rotate through all accents.

### Fonts

- **Titles:** Archivo Black. Fallback: Montserrat ExtraBold.
- **Body:** DM Sans. Fallback: Inter.

| Use | Size and style |
|---|---|
| Cover title | 120 |
| Slide titles | 68–80, Ink Navy, with a colored underline swoosh (Canva element) |
| Eyebrow | DM Sans Bold 22, UPPERCASE, letter spacing 200 |
| Body | 26–28 |
| Big numbers | Archivo Black 88 |

### Recurring motifs

1. **Section eyebrow pill** at the top left, `SECTION 01` to `SECTION 10`. Its color changes per slide.
2. **Content cards:** radius 28, 3 px Ink Navy outline, tinted fill.
3. **Sticker badges:** colored circles, 80 px, with numbers in Archivo Black.
4. **Memphis decor:** 2–3 per slide (squiggle, dot grid, half circle, zigzag).
5. **Page number** in a small Ink Navy pill at the bottom right.

**Transition:** Push.

### Illustration style

One flat/isometric business illustration set. It should include:
- diverse people,
- a café scene,
- laptops,
- charts,
- a rocket,
- a handshake.
