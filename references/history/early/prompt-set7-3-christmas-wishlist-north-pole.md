## 0. Goal

Build a 12-slide Canva presentation (1920 × 1080) called **"Dear North Pole: A Christmas Wishlist That Matters"**. It is a playful guide for kids, families, and classrooms. It covers:
- how to write a thoughtful wishlist
- the four-gift rule
- wishes you can't wrap
- wishes for others
- a sample letter
- a craft
- thank-you notes

**Reference (direction only):** https://www.canva.com/templates/EAG7Eh7nqgs/ ("Red Playful Christmas Wishlist Presentation").

- **Borrow the idea:**
  - a wishlist theme with list-style pages and checkboxes
  - a playful hand-drawn feel
  - red as a key color
- **Build from scratch.** Do not copy any layout, illustration, or text from the reference.

**Must also differ from the earlier Christmas decks in this project:**
- Eco-Friendly Christmas
- Christmas Around the World (world-tour stop tags, dashed travel path, globe)
- Family Fun Guide (mint background, scrapbook polaroids, washi tape)
- Season's Greetings (midnight-blue sky, penguins, frosted-glass panels)

- The other two Christmas decks in this set:

## 1. Borrow vs. must differ

| Borrow | Must differ |
|---|---|
| Wishlist pages with checkboxes | A **"North Pole Post Office" concept**: letters, kraft envelopes, postage-stamp frames, round postmarks, airmail stripes, paper planes, and mail-sorting cubbies |
| Playful hand-drawn feel | Flat, bright illustrations of **diverse kids and cheerful elf mail carriers**. **No** Santa face anywhere |

## 2. Design system

### Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Snow Paper | `#F7F9FF` | Main background |
| Night Ink | `#1F2340` | Text |
| Airmail Red | `#E63946` | Titles, airmail stripes |
| Postal Blue | `#2F6FED` | Accent, airmail stripes |
| Stamp Green | `#2BB673` | Accent |
| Sunflower | `#FFC53D` | Accent, highlights |
| Coral Pink | `#FF7EA6` | Accent |
| Violet Ink | `#7B5BE6` | Accent |
| Kraft | `#E9C89A` | Envelopes, letter paper |

**Background:** Snow Paper on every slide, with a thin airmail-stripe border (red/blue diagonal dashes) along the top or bottom edge.

Every slide has falling snow dots and small paper planes or stars in 3+ palette colors.

### Fonts

| Role | Font | Fallback | Size | Color / style |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Titles | Boogaloo | Baloo 2 ExtraBold | 76–90 | Airmail Red, with a small postmark circle beside the title |
| Body | Nunito SemiBold | Quicksand SemiBold | 26–28 | Night Ink |
| Eyebrow | Nunito ExtraBold | Quicksand Bold | 22 | UPPERCASE, letter spacing 200, palette color |

### Recurring motifs

1. **Stamp cards:** cards in a postage-stamp frame (perforated edge, Canva stamp frame or shape), each a different palette color, with an icon inside.
2. **Envelope cards:** kraft envelopes with a colorful stamp in the corner, holding text.
3. **Postmark badge:** a round wavy-line postmark with `NORTH POLE` and the slide's topic word.
4. **Checkbox list:** rounded checkboxes in palette colors, used on list slides.
5. **Paper-plane trail:** a dotted flight path with a paper plane linking the hero to the text.
6. **Footer:** a tiny envelope icon with the page number on a colored stamp.

**Transition:** Slide ("Meluncur"), right to left, like letters flying in.

### Illustration style

- **One** flat, bright, cute set:
  - diverse kids writing letters and crafting
  - cheerful elf mail carriers with mail bags and sorting cubbies
  - gifts, snowy villages, and paper planes
- The asset scout searches terms like:
  - "kid writing letter flat"
  - "christmas elf mail"
  - "post office illustration"
  - "christmas wishlist"
  - "postage stamp frame"
  - "paper plane"
- **One** rounded filled icon set.

## 3. Slide-by-slide spec

### Builder A

**Slide 1: Cover**
- Eyebrow: `NORTH POLE POST OFFICE`
- Title at (120, 220), width 940, 1 line:
  > Dear North Pole
- Subtitle in a red-and-blue airmail pill:
  > How to Make a Christmas Wishlist That Matters
- Presenter line:
  > Presented by [Your Name] · [Organization]
- Hero (~860, right): cheerful elf mail carriers sorting colorful letters into cubbies, with a big mail bag, flying paper planes and a snowy window.
- Decor: postmarks, stamps, snow dots, paper-plane trail.

**Slide 2: What's a Wishlist?**
- Title: `What's a Wishlist?`
- Statement card (envelope style):
  > A wishlist is a letter of hopes — the things you'd love to have, do and give this Christmas.
- Three stamp cards, each a different color, with an icon:
  - `Things to have`
  - `Things to do`
  - `Things to give`
- Hero (~520, left): a child writing a letter at a desk with crayons and a cup of cocoa.

**Slide 3: How to Write Your Wishlist**
- Title: `How to Write Your Wishlist`
- Five numbered steps, each number on a colored stamp:
  1. Start with "Dear North Pole" and a friendly hello.
  2. Share something you're proud of this year.
  3. List 3–5 wishes, from small to big.
  4. Add one wish for someone else.
  5. Sign it, decorate it and say thank you!
- Hero (~420): an open envelope with a letter, a pencil and a big postmark.

### Builder B

**Slide 4: The Four-Gift Rule**
- Eyebrow `KEEP IT SIMPLE` · Title `The Four-Gift Rule`
- Four stamp cards in a row, each a different color, with an icon:
  - `Something you want`
  - `Something you need`
  - `Something to wear`
  - `Something to read`
- Pill (Stamp Green, white text):
  > Four simple gifts keep Christmas joyful and thoughtful.
- Hero (~420, top right): four wrapped gifts with colorful tags.

**Slide 5: Wishes You Can't Wrap**
- Eyebrow `EXPERIENCE GIFTS` · Title `Wishes You Can't Wrap`
- Six gift-tag shapes (3 × 2), each a different color, with an icon:
  - `A family movie night`
  - `Baking cookies together`
  - `A snowy day at the park`
  - `Learning a new skill`
  - `A day at the museum`
  - `A board-game afternoon`
- Hero (~480, left): a family sledding down a snowy hill together.

**Slide 6: Wishes for Others**
- Eyebrow `GIVING BACK` · Title `Wishes for Others`
- Four envelope cards:
  - `Donate a toy` — Give a new toy to a child who needs one.
  - `Help a neighbor` — Shovel snow or carry groceries.
  - `Say thank you` — Write notes to teachers, doctors or mail carriers.
  - `Share a meal` — Help cook or serve food for someone.
- Hero (~500): kids dropping wrapped toys into a big donation box.

### Builder C

**Slide 7: Smart Wishing Tips**
- Title: `Smart Wishing Tips`
- Three stamp cards:
  - `Be specific` — Add the color, size or title so it's easy to find.
  - `Mix big and small` — Include a few simple wishes along with big dreams.
  - `Stay flexible` — Wishes are hopes, not orders — surprises are fun too!
- Hero (~480): a kid thinking, with thought bubbles showing a kite, a book and a scarf.

**Slide 8: A Sample Letter**
- Title: `A Sample Letter`
- Letter card (kraft paper on a slight tilt, with an envelope behind it):
  > Dear North Pole,
  > This year I learned to ride my bike!
  > I wish for:
  > ☐ A red scarf
  > ☐ A book about space
  > ☐ A kite
  > ☐ A snow day with my family
  > And please bring my grandma a warm blanket.
  > Thank you! — [Child's Name]
- Hero (~460, right): an elf reading a letter with a magnifying glass and a happy smile.

**Slide 9: Craft: Paper Star Wishes**
- Title: `Craft: Paper Star Wishes`
- Four numbered steps, each on a different-colored star:
  1. Cut a star from colored paper.
  2. Write one wish on the back.
  3. Punch a hole and add a ribbon.
  4. Hang it on the tree for everyone to read.
- Hero (~500): kids at a table cutting and decorating colorful paper stars.
