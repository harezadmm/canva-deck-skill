## 0. Goal

Build a 10-slide Canva presentation (1920 × 1080) called **"Business Plan: A Worked Example"**. It is a clearly labeled **sample plan** for a **fictional** business, a mobile bike-repair van called **Wheel Wizard**. On every content slide the viewer sees both **how to write the section** and **a filled-in sample**, so they can copy the method for their own startup.

**Reference (direction only):** https://www.canva.com/templates/EAHIBf4mhEg/ ("Blue White Modern Business Plan Presentation").

- **Borrow the idea:**
  1. an electric **blue + white** mood with a **big, bold, wide headline**;
  2. lots of **white space on a faint grid page**;
  3. a small **oval tag** and a **blue asterisk** accent;
  4. the plan flow: executive summary → problem → solution → market → competitors → business model and marketing → operations → financials → closing.
- **Build from scratch.** Never click "Use this template". Do not copy its pages, elements or text. The reference uses stock photos; this deck uses **flat illustrations only**.

Everything about Wheel Wizard is made up. Mark every sample with `SAMPLE` or `Example only`. No real statistics, no real company names, no real people. Numbers use bracket placeholders such as `[$ amount]`.

**Not a repeat.** This deck must look different from earlier decks (see §1) and from the other two business-plan decks (set9-4 pastel aurora planner, set9-5 blue/orange color-block pitch deck).

## 2. Design system

| White | `#FFFFFF` | Card bodies |

**Color rules:**
- Titles are Ink Navy on the graph paper. On slide 1 the title is white on an Azure block.
- **Tape strips** (semi-transparent washi tape, ~40 × 120 px, tilted) rotate through Hot Pink, Sunny, Aqua and Violet.
- The **asterisk burst** is a different palette color on each slide (cover Lime, 2 Hot Pink, 3 Sunny, 4 Aqua, 5 Violet, 6 Lime, 7 Hot Pink, 8 Sunny, 9 Aqua, 10 Violet).
- White text only on Azure and Violet. Use Ink Navy text on Lime, Pink, Sunny, Aqua and white (contrast ≥ 4.5:1).

### Recurring motifs

1. **Graph-paper page:** faint 48 px grid on every slide (plain lines in the skeleton are fine; they are a layout shape).
2. **Asterisk burst:** a bold multicolor asterisk or star-burst sticker (Canva Elements), 120–200 px, one per slide, color per §2.
3. **Oval SAMPLE tag:** a tilted ellipse outline (Ink Navy, 3 px) with the word `SAMPLE` inside, placed near the title or hero on every slide.
4. **Sticky-note cards with tape:** two cards per content slide (`HOW TO WRITE IT` and `SAMPLE: WHEEL WIZARD`), slightly rotated, with a tape strip at the top.
5. **Wheel Wizard van:** a flat bright van with a bike rack and a star-wheel logo (generic shapes, no brand) that appears on every slide, large as a hero or small beside the title. Same design on every slide.
6. **Footer:** `Wheel Wizard · fictional sample` bottom left (22 px, Ink Navy) and the page number in a small Azure circle bottom right. Not on the cover.

**Transition:** Line wipe ("Usap Garis"), applied to all pages with "Terapkan ke semua halaman". Fallback: Dissolve ("Larut").
