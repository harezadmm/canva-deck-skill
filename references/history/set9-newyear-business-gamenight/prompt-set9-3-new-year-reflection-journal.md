## 0. Goal

Build a 10-slide Canva presentation (1920 × 1080) called **"Look Back, Move Forward: A Year-End Reflection Journal"**. It is a gentle guided workshop for the last week of the year: why reflecting helps, then three steps (Reflect, Release, Reset) with journal prompts, a gratitude jar, a focus-area wheel, intentions, and a letter to your future self.

**Reference (direction only):** https://www.canva.com/templates/EAG8JIdziwI/ ("Gold Modern New Year's Celebration Presentation").

- **Borrow the idea:**
  1. a **warm, glowing, hopeful** mood (the feeling of a first sunrise);
  2. **modern, clean** layouts with big display titles;
  3. a **warm gold-like highlight** (here, flat Honey).
- **Build from scratch.** Never click "Use this template". Do not copy any layout, element, image or text from the reference.

**Not a repeat.** This deck must look different from every earlier deck in this project and from the other set9 decks.

## 2. Design system

### Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cream | `#FFF7EC` | Main background (journal paper) |
| Deep Teal | `#0B5D5E` | Cover and finale backgrounds, card header bars, titles on cream; white text allowed on it |
| Ink Teal | `#12303A` | Text on light fills (text color only) |
| Sunrise Coral | `#FF6B57` | Accent; **Ink text only** (white fails contrast) |
| Honey | `#F7B538` | Accent, highlights, flat gold-like fill; Ink text on it |
| Peach | `#FFC9A8` | Accent, slide 6 background tint; Ink text on it |
| Sage | `#B7DDC5` | Accent, slide 8 background tint `#E6F3EA`; Ink text on it |
| Rose | `#F58FA9` | Accent; Ink text on it |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Paper-note bodies, text on teal |

**Color rules:**
- Backgrounds by slide:

  | Slide | Background |
  |---|---|
  | 1, 10 | Deep Teal with a rising half-sun and soft hills |
  | 6 | Peach tint `#FFE6D6` |
  | 8 | Sage tint `#E6F3EA` |
  | 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9 | Cream |

- Every slide shows **at least 4 palette colors** (notes and pills rotate through Honey, Coral, Peach, Sage, Rose and Teal).
- White text only on Deep Teal. Ink Teal text on every other fill, including Coral.

### Fonts

| Role | Font | Fallback | Size / style | Color |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Titles | Fredoka SemiBold (Bold for the finale) | — (whitelist; re-set if substituted) | 76–100 (finale 150); Fredoka is narrower and rounder than a heavy display face, so a 2-line cover title at 100 fits in 940 px | Deep Teal on cream, white on teal |
| Body | Quicksand Medium | — | 24–28 | Ink Teal |
| Note labels | Quicksand Bold | — | 28 | Ink Teal |
| Eyebrow | Quicksand Bold | — | 22, UPPERCASE, letter spacing 200, inside a Honey or Peach chip | Ink Teal |

Only these 2 families. Both are on the §D whitelist. Max 4 font sizes per slide.

### Recurring motifs

1. **Taped paper notes:** rounded paper cards (radius 16) tilted about 2°, with a strip of **washi tape** on top (Honey / Rose / Sage patterns) and a **dotted writing line** inside. Fill rotates through the accents; bodies are white or cream.
2. **Polaroid frames:** white frames with a thick bottom border holding small illustrations (used for memories on slides 4 and 5).
3. **Rising half-sun (footer):** a small half-sun with rays sits at the bottom edge in the right corner. It sits **lower on slide 1 and rises a little on each slide** until it is fully above the horizon line on slide 10. The page number sits in a Honey circle next to it.
4. **Sun rays and soft hills:** thin rays fan from a half-sun on slides 1 and 10; soft layered hills on slides 1, 3 and 10.
5. **Journal doodles:** tiny stars, dashed trails, leaves, hearts and sparkles in palette colors around heroes and corners, never behind text.

**Transition:** Color Wipe ("Usap Warna"), applied to all pages. If it is missing, use Dissolve ("Larut").

### Illustration style

- One flat, warm, soft-shaded set: diverse people journaling, drinking tea, releasing paper lanterns, walking toward a sunrise; glass jars, envelopes, journals, plants, hills.
- One consistent icon set (bold rounded line icons, filled with palette colors).
- No stock photos, no logos, no brand products.
