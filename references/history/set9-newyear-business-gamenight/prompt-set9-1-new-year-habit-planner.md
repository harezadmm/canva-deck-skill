## 0. Goal

Build a 10-slide Canva presentation (1920 × 1080) called **"Small Habits, Big Year: A 2027 Habit Planner"**. It is a useful New Year deck that shows students and young professionals how to turn big resolutions into small habits that last: pick a word, set a SMART goal, start tiny, use the habit loop, map twelve months, track wins, and bounce back after a slip.

**Reference (direction only):** https://www.canva.com/templates/EAG2mCV_j3Y/ ("Blue and Yellow Textured Happy New Year Presentation").

- **Borrow the idea:**
  1. a bold **blue and yellow** pairing;
  2. a **textured, printed-paper** feel (grain, halftone);
  3. big, chunky New Year display type.
- **Build from scratch.** Never click "Use this template". Do not copy any layout, element, image or text from the reference.

**Not a repeat.** This deck must look different from every earlier deck in this project and from the other set9 decks.

## 2. Design system

### Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cream Paper | `#FFF6DC` | Main background (with grain overlay) |
| Cobalt | `#1F3FBF` | Cover, slide 5 and slide 10 backgrounds, card top strips; white text allowed on it |
| Ink Navy | `#0E1B4D` | Text, titles on light backgrounds (text color only, never a background) |
| Butter Yellow | `#FFD21F` | Highlights, title blocks, slide 4 background; Ink text on it |
| Sky | `#7CC8FF` | Accent; Ink text on it |
| Sky Tint | `#DDF1FF` | Slide 8 background, soft panels |
| Tomato | `#FF6B4A` | Accent; **Ink text only** (white fails contrast) |
| Mint | `#3FD0A3` | Accent; Ink text on it |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Card bodies |

**Color rules:**
- Backgrounds by slide:

  | Slide | Background |
  |---|---|
  | 1, 5, 10 | Cobalt with halftone patches and grain |
  | 4 | Butter Yellow with grain |
  | 8 | Sky Tint with grain |
  | 2, 3, 6, 7, 9 | Cream Paper with grain |

- Each slide shows **at least 4 palette colors** (cards and tiles rotate through Yellow, Sky, Tomato, Mint, Cobalt, white).
- White text only on Cobalt. Ink Navy text on every other fill.
- Grain overlay stays at low opacity so text contrast is never hurt.

### Fonts

| Role | Font | Fallback | Size / style | Color |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Titles | Alfa Slab One | — (whitelist; re-set if substituted) | 76–96 (Thank You 140), key word on a solid color block | Ink Navy on light, white on Cobalt |
| Big letters / numbers | Alfa Slab One | — | 110 (SMART letters, day tabs 40) | Ink Navy or white |
| Body | DM Sans Medium | — | 24–28 (slide 4 cards: 22) | Ink Navy |
| Card labels | DM Sans Bold | — | 28 | Ink Navy |
| Eyebrow | DM Sans Bold | — | 22, UPPERCASE, letter spacing 200, inside a small yellow or sky chip | Ink Navy |

Only these 2 families. Both are on the §D whitelist. Max 4 font sizes per slide.

### Recurring motifs

1. **Calendar-page cards:** white rounded rectangles (radius 20, 3 px Ink outline) with a **colored top strip**, **two small ring holes**, and a small **day-number tab** (Alfa Slab One 40) in a corner. Strip color rotates per card.
2. **Stamp badges:** round badges with a dashed ring and an icon or short word, used as supporting accents (cover, slide 5, slide 10).
3. **Torn-paper edge:** a cream or yellow torn strip along the bottom of slides 1, 5 and 10, and along the top of slide 4.
4. **Grain + halftone:** grain on every background; halftone dot patches in two corners on slides 1, 5, 8 and 10.
5. **Sprout:** a small flat green-and-mint sprout somewhere on every slide (grows bigger as the deck goes on: tiny on 1, a small plant on 7, a tree-sapling on 10).
6. **Footer:** a small yellow **calendar tab** (rounded square, Ink outline) holding the page number, bottom right.

**Transition:** Slide ("Meluncur"), applied to all pages. If it is missing, use Dissolve ("Larut").

### Illustration style

- One flat, friendly, slightly textured set: diverse students and young professionals with planners, sticky notes, calendars, stairs, tracker grids, plants and mugs.
- One consistent icon set (bold rounded outline icons, filled with palette colors).
- No stock photos, no logos, no brand products.

## 4. Speaker notes

- Slides 2–9:
  > General tips for personal planning, not professional advice. Adapt the ideas to your own life.
- Slide 4, add:
  > A SMART goal works best when you can picture exactly what you will do on a normal day.
- Slide 5, add:
  > Tiny habits are starting points. Once a habit feels automatic, you can grow it.
- Slide 9, add:
  > "Never miss twice" is a simple rule of thumb, not a strict rule. Kindness to yourself keeps habits alive.
