## 0. Goal

Build a 10-slide Canva presentation (1920 × 1080) called **"Charades Showdown: A Party Acting Game Night"**. It is a deck the host really runs: two teams act out prompts in silence while teammates guess.

- **How it works:** a 4-step overview with a **30-second timer cue**.
- **Silent signals:** the hand signals that are allowed and what is never allowed.
- **Five category rounds**, each with **6 real prompt cards**: Animals, Jobs, Actions, Feelings and Movie Scenes (the finale, double points).
- **Team rotation:** Team Comedy acts the odd-numbered cards and Team Drama acts the even-numbered cards.
- **Scoring, scoreboard and final awards.**
- **Thank you.**

All prompts are generic. They use no brands, no real people and no famous characters.

**Reference (direction only):** https://www.canva.com/templates/EAHUnMminL8/ ("Colorful Vibrant Bright Retro Playful Checkered Boardgames Game Night Presentation").

- **Borrow the idea:**
  1. **checkerboard backgrounds** in a different bold color per slide;
  2. chunky playful display titles;
  3. glossy **isometric 3D toy objects** as the heroes;
  4. a clear game-night flow: welcome, rules, rounds, winners, see you again.
- **Build from scratch.** Do not copy any page, layout, illustration or text from the reference. Never click "Use this template". The reference's board games (chess, cards, blocks, dominoes, dice) are **not** used. This deck's toys are **acting props**. No photos of people.

**Color rules:**
- Background per slide: 1 Grape · 2 Sun Yellow · 3 Hot Pink · 4 Meadow Green · 5 Cobalt · 6 Tangerine · 7 Sun Yellow · 8 Grape · 9 Cobalt · 10 Hot Pink.
- The checker uses the slide's base color and its "alt" tone (about 10 % lighter, soft).
- **Titles:** White on Grape, Cobalt and Hot Pink. Ink on Sun Yellow, Meadow Green and Tangerine. Always with the 6 px Ink offset shadow (on Ink titles, use a Cream 3 px outline instead of the shadow).
- **Never use the slide's own background color for a tile accent.** Card accents (number squares, header bars, chips, bottom edges) rotate through the **other** palette colors, so **at least 4 palette colors** show on every slide.
- White text only on Grape, Cobalt and Hot Pink fills. Ink text on Cream, Yellow, Meadow and Tangerine fills.

### Recurring motifs

1. **Checker backgrounds:** a two-tone checkerboard (Canva pattern element, recolored to the slide's base and alt tones), tiles about 120 px. A different hue per slide.
2. **Prompt cards:** Cream rounded cards (radius 24) with a 4 px Ink outline and a solid 10 px bottom edge in the accent color, like thick game tiles. Each prompt card has a number square (`1`–`6`) in an accent color.
3. **30-second timer bar:** on every round slide (4–8), a bar split in three segments with an hourglass icon: Meadow `30 · GO!` → Sun Yellow `20 · HURRY` → Hot Pink `10 · LAST CHANCE`. Plain shapes with Ink text (white on Hot Pink).
4. **Team chips:** `TEAM COMEDY` (Hot Pink, white text) and `TEAM DRAMA` (Cobalt, white text) with a small mask icon, used on slides 7, 9 and in the card labels.
5. **Isometric props:** glossy theater masks, hourglass, megaphone, clapperboard, top hat, spotlight, star trophy, from Canva Elements, one consistent style.
6. **Spotlight beams:** soft translucent cones of light (Cream at 25 %) falling on the heroes.
7. **Poker-style chip footer:** a round chip (dashed edge ring) in an accent color holding the page number, bottom right.
8. **Sparkles and confetti squares:** tiny squares, stars and sparkles in palette colors near heroes and corners.

**Transition:** Color Wipe ("Usap Warna"), applied to all pages. Fallback if missing: Dissolve ("Larut").

## 4. Speaker notes

- Slide 2:
  > Set up: two teams, a 30-second timer (an hourglass or a phone), and paper for scoring. The actor reads the prompt from the screen while everyone else looks away. To hide prompts completely, print slides 4–8 as cards instead. Start each turn with "Ready, set, act!" and call "Ten seconds left!" at the 20-second mark.
- Slide 3, add:
  > Demonstrate each signal once before you start. Beginners can skip the "sounds like" signal.
- Slides 4–8:
  > Team Comedy acts cards 1, 3 and 5; Team Drama acts cards 2, 4 and 6. Use a new actor every time. With three teams, give each team two cards: 1 and 4, 2 and 5, 3 and 6. Swap in your own prompts to fit your audience.
- Slide 8, add:
  > Double points: 4 points if guessed in time, 2 points in the last 10 seconds.
- Slide 9, add:
  > Scoring: 2 points if guessed in time, 1 point in the last 10 seconds, 0 if time runs out; Movie Scenes count double. Maximum per team with two teams: 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 + 12 = 36. Tie-breaker: one extra prompt for each team; the fastest correct guess wins.
- Slide 10, add:
  > Thank everyone, and ask which category they want next time.
