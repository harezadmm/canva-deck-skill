## 0. Goal

Build a 10-slide Canva presentation (1920 × 1080) called **"The Countdown Party Playbook"**. It is a practical guide to hosting a fun, safe New Year's Eve party at home: theme, timeline, invitations, food, music, games, the midnight moment, and party safety.

**Reference (direction only):** https://www.canva.com/templates/EAG2gtxCyL0/ ("Purple and Gold Modern New Year's Party Celebration Presentation").

- **Borrow the idea:**
  1. a **purple-led, modern party** mood;
  2. a **warm gold accent** used as a bright highlight;
  3. an energetic, celebratory flow from invitation to midnight.
- **Build from scratch.** Never click "Use this template". Do not copy any layout, element, image or text from the reference.

**Not a repeat.** This deck must look different from every earlier deck in this project and from the other set9 decks.

## 2. Design system

### Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Grape Night | `#2E1065` | Main background, as a subtle 2-stop gradient `#2E1065` → `#4C1D95` (top-left to bottom-right); white text allowed |
| Ink Grape | `#1B0B3A` | Text on light fills (text color only) |
| Disco Magenta | `#D6006F` | Accent, cover pill, countdown badge; white text allowed on it |
| Party Gold | `#FFB800` | Accent, highlights; Ink text on it |
| Aqua Pop | `#22D3EE` | Accent; Ink text on it |
| Lime Zing | `#B8F23C` | Accent; Ink text on it |
| Warm Cream | `#FFF4DB` | Slides 5 and 8 backgrounds, card bodies |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Text on dark, ticket bodies |

**Color rules:**
- Backgrounds by slide:

  | Slide | Background |
  |---|---|
  | 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10 | Grape Night gradient |
  | 5, 8 | Warm Cream (light rest slides; titles in Ink Grape) |

- Ticket stubs rotate through Party Gold, Aqua Pop, Disco Magenta and Lime Zing, so **at least 4 accents** show on every slide.
- White text only on Grape Night and Disco Magenta. Ink Grape text on Gold, Aqua, Lime and Cream.
- Never put text on top of busy decoration (disco-ball sparkles, confetti stay at the edges).

### Fonts

| Role | Font | Fallback | Size / style | Color |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Titles | Lilita One | — (whitelist; re-set if substituted) | 76–96 (finale 140), no outline | White on dark, Ink Grape on cream |
| Ticket headings | Lilita One | — | 36 | Ink Grape (white on Magenta) |
| Body | Poppins | — | 24–26 | Ink Grape on tickets, white on dark |
| Eyebrow | Poppins Bold | — | 22, UPPERCASE, letter spacing 200, inside a Party Gold chip | Ink Grape |
| Countdown numbers | Lilita One | — | 44 inside the footer badge | White on Magenta |

Only these 2 families. Both are on the §D whitelist. Max 4 font sizes per slide.

### Recurring motifs

1. **Ticket-stub cards:** rounded rectangles with a **half-circle notch** on each side and a **dotted perforation line** near one end. Fill rotates Gold / Aqua / Magenta / Lime (Cream on dark slides when more contrast is wanted).
2. **Hanging disco ball:** a flat disco ball on a string in the top-right corner of every slide (large on the cover, small elsewhere), with a few sparkle dots around it.
3. **Bunting:** a thin string of small triangle flags along the very top edge in the four accents (omit where the disco ball sits).
4. **Confetti and streamers:** palette-colored dots, stars and curly streamers in the corners, never behind text.
5. **Countdown badge (footer):** a round Disco Magenta badge, bottom right, with the countdown number (white, Lilita One 44): slide 1 shows `10`, slide 2 `9`, … slide 9 `2`, slide 10 `1`.

**Transition:** Stack ("Tumpuk"), applied to all pages. If it is missing, use Dissolve ("Larut").

### Illustration style

- One flat, bright, playful house-party set: diverse friends in party hats, a disco ball, balloons, a snack table, vinyl records, phones with playlists, polaroid-style photos.
- Drinks appear only as sparkling juice or mocktails in plain glasses (no bottles, no champagne).
- One consistent icon set (bold rounded line icons, filled with palette colors).
- No stock photos, no logos, no brand products.
