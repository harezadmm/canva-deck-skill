## 0. Goal

Build a 10-slide Canva presentation (1920 × 1080) called **"Turn Your Idea Into a Business Plan"**. It is a friendly, educational guide for first-time founders, students and side-hustlers: what each section of a business plan is for and what to write in it.

**Reference (direction only):** https://www.canva.com/templates/EAGwmXiQrgA/ ("Pink Purple and White Modern Business Plan Presentation").

- **Borrow the idea:**
  1. a soft **pink / purple / coral aurora mood** (blurry gradient light on a clean light page);
  2. a **wide, modern headline** over calm white space;
  3. the classic plan flow: executive summary → problem and solution → market → competition → marketing → operations and team → finances → next steps.
- **Build from scratch.** Never click "Use this template". Do not copy its pages, elements or text.

The topic angle is **creative / lifestyle small business** (a florist, a baker, a maker, a home café). The only business named is a **made-up example** and every example is labeled `Example only`. No real statistics, no real company names, no real people.

**Not a repeat.** This deck must look different from earlier decks (see §1) and from the other two business-plan decks (set9-5 blue/orange color-block pitch deck, set9-6 white graph-paper sample plan).

## 2. Design system

### Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Orchid Mist | `#FBF5FF` | Background (with 2–3 aurora blobs per slide) |
| Ink Plum | `#2B1A4A` | Text, titles |
| Deep Orchid | `#7A45D6` | Main brand color, step badges; white text allowed on it |
| Coral | `#FF7A66` | Accent, problem, energy; Ink text on it |
| Bubblegum | `#FF8FC7` | Accent; Ink text on it |
| Periwinkle | `#7C8BFF` | Accent; Ink text on it |
| Aqua Mint | `#5FE0C4` | Solutions, growth; Ink text on it |
| Lemon | `#FFD66B` | Highlights, tips; Ink text on it |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Card bodies |

**Color rules:**
- **Aurora blobs** are soft, blurred gradients (Orchid → Bubblegum → Coral, and Periwinkle → Aqua). Put them behind content, never behind small text. Each slide uses a different mix so no two pages look identical.
- Each card header chip or step badge uses one accent from the table; rotate through the palette so that **at least 4 accents** show on every slide.
- White text only on Deep Orchid. Use Ink Plum text on every other color (contrast ≥ 4.5:1).
- Gradient squiggle underline under titles: Deep Orchid → Bubblegum → Coral.

Only these 2 families. Both are on the §D whitelist.

### Recurring motifs

1. **Aurora blobs:** big soft blurred gradient shapes in the corners and behind heroes (Canva Elements, recolored to the palette).
2. **STEP pill:** a Deep Orchid pill with a small arrow circle, top right on every slide: `STEP 01 / 10` … `STEP 10 / 10`. It doubles as the page number.
3. **Idea Bulb buddy:** a cute flat lightbulb character (face, little arms) that appears on every slide, as the hero accessory or peeking beside the title. Same design on every slide; only pose and accessory change.
4. **Frosted sticker cards:** white rounded cards (radius 32, 3 px lilac outline, soft shadow) with a round numbered badge on the top-left corner.
5. **Gradient squiggle underline** under every title (Canva squiggle or wave line, recolored).
6. **Sparkle confetti:** tiny four-point sparkles, dots and little hearts in palette colors around heroes and corners.

**Transition:** Dissolve ("Larut"), applied to all pages with "Terapkan ke semua halaman". Soft, to match the aurora mood.
