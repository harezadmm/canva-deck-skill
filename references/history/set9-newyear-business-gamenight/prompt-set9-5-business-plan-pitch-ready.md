## 0. Goal

Build a 10-slide Canva presentation (1920 × 1080) called **"The Pitch-Ready Business Plan"**. It is an educational guide for founders, students and managers who must present a plan to investors, a bank or a leadership team: what each section must answer and how to say it.

**Reference (direction only):** https://www.canva.com/templates/EAGugioIyeo/ ("Blue and Orange Modern Business Plan Presentation").

- **Borrow the idea:**
  1. a confident **blue + orange** corporate mood with **bold color blocks**;
  2. **alternating dark and light slides** for rhythm;
  3. a bold, wide headline in heavy sans type;
  4. the pitch flow: executive summary → vision and mission → goals → market → competition → business model → team → financials → closing and next steps.
- **Build from scratch.** Never click "Use this template". Do not copy its pages, elements or text.

The topic angle is **corporate / investor pitch**. Examples are generic and labeled `Example only`. No real statistics, no real company names, no real people. Where a name is needed, use a bracket placeholder such as `[Your Startup]`.

**Not a repeat.** This deck must look different from earlier decks (see §1) and from the other two business-plan decks (set9-4 pastel aurora planner, set9-6 white graph-paper sample plan).

## 1. Borrow vs. must differ

**Color rules:**
- White text only on Ultramarine and Charcoal. Use Charcoal text on Cream, Tangerine, Sunflower, Lilac and Mint (contrast ≥ 4.5:1).
- On dark slides, cards are white or Sunflower/Tangerine blocks so the page stays colorful.
- The **key word** of each title can be set in Tangerine on dark slides only.

### Recurring motifs

1. **Geometric tile cluster:** a 3 × 3 block of quarter-circles, half-circles and squares in palette colors, in one corner of every slide (position changes per slide).
2. **Block cards:** solid-color rounded rectangles (radius 24, no outline) in Sunflower, Tangerine, Ultramarine, Lilac, Mint or white, with a bold label and an icon.
3. **Rocket and chart pair:** a flat rocket and a flat rising bar chart appear as small illustrations on most slides (and as the cover hero).
4. **Number bubbles:** big Rubik numerals `01`, `02`, `03` on cards and steps.
5. **Orbit dots:** thin circle rings with small colored dots on dark slides.
6. **Footer:** `[Your Business Name]` bottom left in small caps (22 px) and the page number in a small Tangerine circle bottom right. Not on the cover.

**Transition:** Slide ("Meluncur"), applied to all pages with "Terapkan ke semua halaman". Fallback: Dissolve ("Larut").

  > The table is an example only. Compare real competitors honestly and avoid claims you cannot support.

  > The charts are decoration only. Build real forecasts in a spreadsheet and ask an accountant to review them.
